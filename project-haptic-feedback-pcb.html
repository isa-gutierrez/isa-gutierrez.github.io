Project · Neurotech@Berkeley / 2025

Haptic feedback communication PCB

Modular system combining tactile and magnetic sensing to enable haptic feedback and sensory augmentation in prosthetics and robotics.

My role

Hardware design

Focus

Embedded systems / sensing

Tools & methods

KiCad · STM32 · I²C · I³C · SPI · Schematic capture

What I worked on

  • Developed a modular haptic feedback system, leading research and topology design for a low-noise, power-efficient architecture integrating an STM32 with barometric and magnetic sensors.
  • Created full schematics and layouts of an STM32-based PCB, efficiently bussing power and implementing I²C, I³C, and SPI protocols for automated multi-sensor data acquisition and reliable system integration.
