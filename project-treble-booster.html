Custom analog treble booster

A precision analog treble booster built around an LM741 op-amp, validated in LTspice and on a physical prototype.

Tools & methods

What I worked on

  • Engineered a precision analog treble booster utilizing an LM741 op-amp, performing hand calculations and optimizing RC networks for high-frequency tone shaping across the audible spectrum.
  • Validated through LTspice simulation and a physical prototype.

Result

Performance came within 5% of target specifications, showing strong correlation between theoretical, simulated, and real-world behavior.
