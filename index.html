EECS · UC Berkeley

Hi, I'm Isa.

I'm an Electrical Engineering and Computer Science student at UC Berkeley, graduating in May 2028. I love chips, from how they're fabricated to how they're designed, and I'm currently doing research in nanofabrication.

I've interned at KLA and EUV Tech, but I'm drawn to hard hardware problems in any industry. I've built control systems for climate tech, sensing hardware for automotive, and an ASIC from RTL to layout, and I'm still exploring where I want to focus.
