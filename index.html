03 / A little about me

Curious about the whole system.

I'm studying Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Berkeley. My work tends to sit at the intersection of circuits, physical systems, and the software needed to make them useful.

I enjoy the details of a board layout, but also the bigger questions: what should we measure, what can fail, and how do we know a system works outside the lab? That perspective has taken me from semiconductor tools and high-vacuum chambers to underwater robotics and sensing for agriculture.

Earlier, I led Geneseas, an all-female underwater robotics team of 25. Teaching CAD and soldering while building competition hardware helped shape the way I collaborate today.
