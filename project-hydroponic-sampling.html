Automated hydroponic water sampling

A compact control system for rapid water-quality analysis in hydroponic growing systems.

Space for project photograph or diagram
My role

Electrical Engineering Consultant

Focus

Embedded systems

Tools & methods

KiCad · ESP32 · C++ · Pump control

What I worked on

  • Designed an ESP32-based control PCB with power regulation, pump drivers, leak sensing, and sensor interfacing.
  • Built the electrical architecture for extracting, routing, analyzing, and returning water samples.
  • Worked with the mechanical team on a waterproof enclosure and system integration.

Result

A pilot-ready prototype enabled automated sampling cycles of approximately one minute.
