Electronics for an underwater ROV

More reliable electronics and communication for a competition underwater vehicle.

Space for project photograph or diagram
My role

Executive Lead and Principal Engineer

Focus

Robotics / documentation

Tools & methods

PCB design · Powerline communication · Technical writing

What I worked on

  • Consolidated 15 electronic components onto a 58 × 49 mm two-layer Raspberry Pi PCB to improve wiring and serviceability.
  • Adapted commercial powerline communication boards for a 12 V DC tether, then verified 128 Mbps throughput with iPerf.
  • Led a 25-person all-female team and co-wrote a 25-page technical report documenting design and testing.

Result

The team's 2024 technical documentation earned first place at the MATE ROV World Championships.
