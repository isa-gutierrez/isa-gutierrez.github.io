Robotics · Geneseas / 2023–24

Electronics for an underwater ROV

More reliable electronics and communication for a competition underwater vehicle.

My role

Executive Lead and Principal Engineer

Focus

Robotics / documentation

Tools & methods

Altium · PCB design · Rapid prototyping · Powerline communication · iPerf · Technical writing

What I worked on

  • Consolidated 15 electronic components onto a 58 × 49 mm two-layer Raspberry Pi PCB to improve wiring and serviceability.
  • Created system block diagrams and analyzed component specifications and datasheets to develop PCB schematics that efficiently bus power and I²C to the ROV system.
  • Tested the efficiency of electronic components (RPi 4B power supply, PWM drivers for DC motors, and an electromagnetic relay) for mission tools.
  • Developed a powerline communication (PLC) system over two 12 V power cables for ethernet networking and TCP/IP communication, streamlining hardware requirements while waterproofing the ethernet cables (412 bar).
  • Tested that the PLC system had enough bandwidth to carry visual output from four digital cameras and input data from the control stick, and verified 128 Mbps throughput with iPerf.
  • Diagnosed power distribution issues on the PLC board with a multimeter and rewired it so the original AC system could accommodate a DC system.
  • Led and mentored an all-female team of 25, teaching CAD, soldering, and engineering principles, and led the team to the World Championships three times, consistently ranking top five overall. Co-wrote a 25-page technical report documenting design and testing.

Result

The team's 2024 technical documentation earned first place at the MATE ROV World Championships.
