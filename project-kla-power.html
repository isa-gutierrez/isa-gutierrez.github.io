Power electronics for semiconductor inspection

Evaluation hardware for the high-power DC-DC converters behind semiconductor inspection camera systems.

Space for project photograph or diagram
My role

Electrical Design Engineering Intern

Focus

Power electronics

Tools & methods

OrCAD X · LTspice · I²C · PMBus

What I worked on

  • Designed a 600+ W evaluation PCB in OrCAD X with configurable I²C and PMBus interfaces for converter communication and control.
  • Simulated low-pass filter networks in LTspice for 30+ A applications, targeting noise attenuation above 10 kHz.
  • Evaluated and characterized converters before their integration into a broader camera power supply system.

Result

The evaluation and filter work supported integration of high-power converters into imaging electronics used for wafer inspection.
