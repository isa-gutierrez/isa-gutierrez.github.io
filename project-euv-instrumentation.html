Internship · EUV Tech / 2025–26

Instrumentation for EUV systems

Measurement boards and automated hardware for extreme ultraviolet tools.

Space for project photograph or diagram
My role

Electrical Engineer Intern

Focus

PCB design / systems

Tools & methods

KiCad · Photodiodes · High vacuum · System integration

What I worked on

  • Designed a photoconductivity PCB in KiCad that increased maximum bias voltage from 25 V to 200 V for photodiode measurements.
  • Developed a photodiode emulator PCB to generate precise current pulses for preamp tuning, testing, and calibration.
  • Engineered the electrical system of an automated high-vacuum chamber for residual gas analysis and semiconductor optics cleaning, then carried out a successful pumpdown test.

Result

The work connected board-level design with the test and chamber systems needed to characterize optics and electronics.
