Internship · EUV Tech / 2025–26

Instrumentation for EUV systems

Measurement boards and automated hardware for extreme ultraviolet tools.

Space for project photograph or diagram
My role

Electrical Engineer Intern

Focus

PCB design / systems

Tools & methods

KiCad · SolidWorks · LabVIEW · NI CompactRIO · Photodiodes · High vacuum

What I worked on

  • Designed photoconductivity PCB using KiCad, multiplying max bias voltage 8x (from 25 V to 200 V) to improve response time and sensitivity of photodiode dark current and laser intensity measurements.
  • Developed photodiode emulator PCB to generate precise current pulses to tune, test, and calibrate preamps for use in extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography and metrology tools.
  • Engineered the complete electrical system of an automated high-vacuum chamber (10⁻⁶ mbar) for residual gas analysis and semiconductor optics cleaning in a cleanroom environment. Created SolidWorks schematics, integrated all pumps, gauges, and valves, and executed a successful pumpdown test.
  • Wrote and integrated LabVIEW programs with NI CompactRIO to perform data acquisition and verify RS-232 and RS-485 communication of laser power and comms PCBs for semiconductor pellicle conditioning.
  • Built and deployed a multi-modal inspection system that performs real-time edge detection with Keyence cameras, a laser profiler, and NI CompactRIO to validate copper tape XYZ-positioning and maintenance within a 70% relative consistency tolerance across 60 mm tape segments.

Result

The work connected board-level design with the test and chamber systems needed to characterize optics and electronics.
